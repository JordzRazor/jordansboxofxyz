What this place is for

A personal shopfront, not a product. A handful of things to look at when the day has a spare corner in it, and a few tools that came out of making them.

Nothing here asks you to sign in or pay attention for long. Close the tab whenever.

  • Gentle on old laptops: capped particle counts, and everything pauses when the tab is hidden.
  • WebGPU pieces fall back to WebGL on their own. Nothing just breaks.
  • No accounts, no tracking, no dark patterns.
